Breakfast Recipes

Greek Yogurt + Berries + Oats

Ingredients:
  • 1 cup Greek yogurt
  • ½–1 cup mixed berries
  • 1–2 tbsp oats or granola
Instructions:
  • Add yogurt to a bowl.
  • Top with berries.
  • Sprinkle oats or granola and serve.

Protein Shake + Banana

Ingredients:
  • 1 scoop protein powder
  • 250–300 ml water or milk
  • 1 banana
Instructions:
  • Add protein powder and liquid to shaker.
  • Shake well.
  • Eat banana on the side.

Oatmeal + Protein + Fruit

Ingredients:
  • ½ cup oats
  • 250 ml water or milk
  • ½–1 scoop protein powder
  • ½–1 cup berries or fruit
Instructions:
  • Cook oats with water or milk.
  • Stir in protein powder off the heat.
  • Top with fruit and serve.

Cottage Cheese + Fruit

Ingredients:
  • 1 cup cottage cheese
  • ½–1 cup sliced fruit
Instructions:
  • Put cottage cheese in a bowl.
  • Add fruit on top and eat cold.

Eggs + Whole-Grain Toast

Ingredients:
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 slice whole-grain bread
  • Salt, pepper, optional butter
Instructions:
  • Cook eggs (scrambled, fried, or boiled).
  • Toast bread.
  • Serve together, season eggs.

Smoothie (Protein + Banana + Berries)

Ingredients:
  • 1 scoop protein powder
  • 1 banana
  • ½ cup berries
  • 250–300 ml water or milk
Instructions:
  • Add all ingredients to blender.
  • Blend until smooth and drink.

Yogurt + Granola

Ingredients:
  • 1 cup Greek yogurt
  • ¼–½ cup granola
Instructions:
  • Add yogurt to bowl.
  • Top with granola and serve.
Lunch Recipes

Grilled Chicken Salad

Ingredients:
  • 1 chicken breast
  • 2 cups mixed greens
  • ½ cup chopped veggies
  • 1–2 tbsp light vinaigrette
Instructions:
  • Cook chicken and slice.
  • Add greens and veggies to a bowl.
  • Top with chicken and drizzle vinaigrette.

Turkey Wrap

Ingredients:
  • 1 whole-grain wrap
  • 3–4 slices turkey
  • Lettuce, tomato
  • Mustard or light spread
Instructions:
  • Lay out wrap.
  • Add turkey, lettuce, tomato, and mustard.
  • Roll tightly and slice if desired.

Tuna Salad Bowl

Ingredients:
  • 1 can tuna (drained)
  • 2 cups greens
  • ½ cup cucumber/veggies
  • Lemon juice, pepper
Instructions:
  • Mix tuna with lemon juice and pepper.
  • Put greens and veggies in a bowl.
  • Top with tuna and serve.

Chicken + Quinoa Bowl

Ingredients:
  • 1 chicken breast
  • ½–1 cup cooked quinoa
  • 1 cup spinach or greens
  • 1–2 tbsp light dressing
Instructions:
  • Cook quinoa and set aside.
  • Cook chicken and slice.
  • Combine quinoa, greens, and chicken in a bowl.
  • Add dressing and toss.

Lentil Soup + Side Salad

Ingredients:
  • 1 bowl lentil soup
  • 1–2 cups mixed greens
  • ½ cup veggies
  • 1–2 tbsp light dressing
Instructions:
  • Heat lentil soup.
  • Make salad with greens and veggies.
  • Dress salad lightly and serve with soup.

Salmon Salad

Ingredients:
  • 1 salmon fillet
  • 2 cups greens
  • ½ cup cucumber/veggies
  • Lemon dressing
Instructions:
  • Cook salmon and let cool slightly.
  • Add greens and veggies to a bowl.
  • Flake or slice salmon on top.
  • Add lemon dressing.

Chicken Caesar Salad

Ingredients:
  • 1 chicken breast
  • 2 cups romaine lettuce
  • 1–2 tbsp light Caesar dressing
  • Optional: small sprinkle parmesan
Instructions:
  • Cook chicken and slice.
  • Toss romaine with dressing.
  • Top with chicken and parmesan.

Dinner Recipes

Salmon + Roasted Vegetables

Ingredients:
  • 1 salmon fillet
  • 2 cups mixed vegetables
  • 1–2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper
Instructions:
  • Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
  • Place salmon on baking sheet; season.
  • Toss veggies with olive oil, salt, pepper; spread around salmon.
  • Bake 15–20 minutes until salmon is cooked and veggies are tender.

Chicken Stir-Fry

Ingredients:
  • 1 chicken breast, sliced
  • 2 cups mixed veggies
  • 1–2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tsp oil
Instructions:
  • Heat oil in pan.
  • Cook chicken until browned.
  • Add veggies and stir-fry 5–7 minutes.
  • Add soy sauce, toss, and serve.

Beef + Roasted Vegetables

Ingredients:
  • 4–5 oz lean beef strips
  • 2 cups mixed vegetables
  • 1–2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper
Instructions:
  • Roast veggies at 400°F for ~20 minutes with oil, salt, pepper.
  • Pan-sear beef strips until cooked.
  • Serve beef over roasted veggies.

Baked Cod + Vegetables

Ingredients:
  • 1 cod fillet
  • 2 cups vegetables
  • 1–2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, lemon
Instructions:
  • Preheat oven to 400°F.
  • Place cod on baking sheet; season and drizzle with oil.
  • Add veggies around fish, toss with oil.
  • Bake 12–15 minutes until fish flakes easily.

Chicken Fajita Plate

Ingredients:
  • 1 chicken breast, sliced
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • ½ onion, sliced
  • Fajita seasoning
Instructions:
  • Cook chicken in pan until browned.
  • Add peppers and onions.
  • Sprinkle fajita seasoning and cook until veggies are tender.
  • Serve on a plate (no tortilla needed).

Turkey Meatballs + Vegetables

Ingredients:
  • 8 oz ground turkey
  • Salt, pepper, herbs
  • 2 cups vegetables
  • 1–2 tbsp olive oil
Instructions:
  • Mix turkey with seasoning; form small meatballs.
  • Bake at 400°F for 15–20 minutes.
  • Roast veggies separately with oil, salt, pepper.
  • Serve meatballs with veggies; add tomato sauce if you like.

Shrimp Stir-Fry

Ingredients:
  • 4–5 oz shrimp, peeled
  • 1–2 cups broccoli or mixed veggies
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Garlic, ginger, soy sauce (optional)
Instructions:
  • Heat oil in pan.
  • Cook shrimp until pink.
  • Add garlic and ginger, then veggies.
  • Stir-fry 3–4 minutes; add a splash of soy if desired.
